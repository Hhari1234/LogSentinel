About the Analysis

LogSentinel analyzes application log files and identifies errors, warnings, frequent issues, and repeated-error patterns that may indicate potential incidents. All metrics are computed from the most recently uploaded and analyzed log file.

Important: Detected incidents are pattern-based findings, not confirmed root causes. They indicate suspicious repetition that warrants investigation.
